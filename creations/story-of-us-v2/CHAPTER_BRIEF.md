# Shared production brief

Read ANIMATION_GUIDE.md and STORYBOARD.md first, then src/scenes/world.js and c00_open.js. Canvas 1920×1080, safe action area y<930; lyric paper band y=962–1080. Song duration 283.4, BPM 68, beat offset .391. Palette and all props are in window.V. Same terracotta A and rose B with flower. Never change shared files or the original V1.

Your sole scene file is an IIFE. Register each shot with `V.register(start,label,lens,fn,transition,reads)`; fn(t,localTime,duration) draws the whole frame. lens = wide, medium, close, insert. Transition = wipe, iris, fade, cut (intentional action/match cut). Use cut for inserts/reaction cuts and variety, wipe at chapter boundaries; don't wipe every shot. Global assemble.js determines durations from the sorted starts. Do not call shots() yourself.

API: V.E(t,a,b) eased progress; V.begin(l,d,cx=960,cy=540,z=1,pushIn=.025), camEnd() pair. V.bg(kind,t,warm) kinds room, desk, garden, river, street, station, night. River has water at y~640–810 and WALKWAY at y>=813. Stage feet at y~870–920. Room window on RIGHT at x1330; shelf left. V.actor(id,x,y,u,t,l,emotionKeys,options) uses engine clawd. Sizes u32–45 medium, u60–80 close, u18–25 wide. Default emotes disabled to avoid emoji acting. Options standard clawd: aL/aR, lookX/Y, turn() / view, flip, armL/armR hook. Hook draws in arm-tip local coordinates +x outward, left hand reflects x (scale -1,1), retaining upright y. Held props must touch hooks; show actions, not floating props. Motion reaction sequence must have timed reads.

V primitives: box(x,y,w,h,col,sw), shape(points,col,sw,ink), oval(x,y,rx,ry,col,sw), stroke(points,sw,col); V.table(x,y,w), V.bench(x,y,s), V.foreground(t), V.tree(x,y,s,t,season0to3). Reused props: mug(x,y,s,t,col), house(x,y,s,t,lit), umbrella(x,y,s,t,tilt), flower(x,y,s,t), sprout(x,y,s,growth,t), suitcase(x,y,s,t), pencil(x,y,s,rot), redThread(points,sw). V.book(x,y,s,t,stage=0,close=0) centered book 850x450: stage0 one portrait, 1 two portraits, 2 tea/flower/thread, 3 house/umbrella, 4 suitcase, increasing visible page stack; close=0 open 1 closed. V.photo(x,y,s,t,kind=0) postcard (0 street,1 umbrella,2 house). V.envelope(x,y,s,rot). Add private helpers as needed. Don't use old V.backdrop or frameFinish; these are legacy vertical helpers.

Pure deterministic functions of t, use hash() and boilSeed() per independent object; no stateful frame evolution. No plain p5 shapes: paint/inkLine through V helpers. Avoid much >200 primitives/frame, minimize p5.brush fill effects. Every chapter must contain distinct staging/lenses: single actor reactions, detail actions, wide environment and back/side views. No repeated sequence of two front-facing actors smiling with a heart. Supporting foreground and environmental details must explain setting, not clutter.

Review: write your own temporary review HTML if needed by copying studio.html and replacing irrelevant script tags, or use main studio.html once file is syntactically valid. Main scene may include unfinished other chapters, don't alter it. `node render.mjs --sheet=<times> --cols=3 --w=480 --out=out/check/<chapter>.jpg`. Chrome launch needs sandbox escalation with require_escalated (approved for local renderer). Review representative stills, then a sheet per shot with start/mid/end, key motion strips and face/prop crops. Inspect the images. Return actual time ranges, changes, any problems. Do not render full film or edit shared API. Save shot/read notes in a chapter-specific .md. Send parent readiness to inspect before final.
